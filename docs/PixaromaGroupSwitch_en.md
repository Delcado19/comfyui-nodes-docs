Group Switch Pixaroma - a compact panel of on/off switches for your Pixaroma Groups. Each switch mutes or bypasses every node inside that group, so you can turn whole sections of a workflow on and off without wiring anything.

Open the panel (the gear, or right-click the node) to choose whether this switch mutes or bypasses, which groups it controls (all of them, or a hand-picked set you search and sort), and the switching rule (any number on, only one on at a time, or always keep one on).

Drop several of these for different jobs - one to mute, one to bypass. They stay in sync with each other and with the group's own header buttons, because they all read and set the live group state.
